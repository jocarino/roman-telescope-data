Full spectrum — the colour of the planet's whole modelled reflected-light spectrum, 380–780 nm.

Seen from Roman — the same planet rebuilt from only the {{ g('roman-4-band', 'three bands') }} the {{ g('coronagraph', 'Roman Coronagraph') }} formally supports (575, 730 and 825 nm). Its bluest band is 575 nm, so nothing below that is sampled at all: the model holds the reflectance flat there and the blues collapse toward grey and orange. That gap is Roman's, not the planets'.

Switching re-keys everything colour-derived — the colour filter, the brightness sort, the similar-colour sort. {{ stats.family_shift_pct }}% of planets land in a different colour family between the two views.

Both are {{ g('modelled') }} predictions, not photographs.
