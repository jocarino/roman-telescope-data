Your sky tonight

every host star above your horizon, right now
The idea

Every planet in this catalog orbits a real star at a real spot in Earth's sky. This page filters the whole map down to what is over your head right now: set your location (or let the browser set it), and the chart shades away everything below your horizon. Hover a star to identify it; click to see the worlds it carries.

Honesty

What you can ever see is the star — no exoplanet is visible to the eye through any telescope. "Naked eye" uses the dark-sky limit (V ≤ 6.5); city skies show far less. Time comes from your device clock; your east–west position is estimated from its time zone unless you press "use my location" (read once, used only on this device — this site has no server). Stars with no measured brightness (microlensing hosts) are never listed as visible.
